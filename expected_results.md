# Expected Results

The proposed DLP approach is expected to improve the identification of suspicious data-transfer or insider-threat activities.

Expected findings include:
1. Identification of useful behavioural and data-related indicators.
2. Detection of potentially risky activities across relevant channels.
3. Reduction of false positives compared with a simple rule-only approach, if supported by experiments.
4. A practical evaluation of detection performance and real-time feasibility.
5. Identification of remaining limitations such as class imbalance, limited realistic data, and evasion behaviour.

These are expected outcomes, not confirmed experimental results.
